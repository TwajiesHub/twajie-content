# Sources: why_is_it_called_obalende_v1.mp4 (checked Nov 17, 2026)

Script: "Why is it called Obalende? It's short for a Yoruba saying: Ibi ti Oba le wa de. Where the king chased us to. Soldiers of the Royal West African Frontier Force, mostly Hausa men, once camped where King's College stands today. When that land was needed, the Oba of Lagos pushed to move them. So they named their new home with that saying. Today, it's famous for Suya Junction. Follow for more Lagos names explained."

## 1. Name = contraction of the Yoruba saying "Ibi ti Oba le wa de", "where the king chased/drove us to" (surprising claim, 3 sources)
- Wikipedia, Obalende: "a contraction of the Yoruba saying Ibi ti Oba le wa de, meaning 'Where the king chased us to'"; "the RWAFF men named the place Ibi ti Ọba lé wà dé ... 'the place where the king drove us to'." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obalende
- Daily Trust, "The Hausas of Obalende" (interview with Sarkin Hausawa Sule Garba Dayi): "people began to say of the new settlement 'Ibi ti Oba le wa de,' Yoruba words meaning 'this is where the Oba drove us.'" https://dailytrust.com/the-hausas-of-obalende/
- World and City Maps, Obalende: "The name is a contraction of the Yoruba phrase Ibi ti Oba le wa de, which translates to 'Where the king chased us to.'" https://worldandcitymaps.com/africa/obalende (derivative of Wikipedia; supporting only)

## 2. Royal West African Frontier Force, predominantly Hausa men, first camped on the King's College land
- Wikipedia: "The Royal West African Frontier Force (RWAFF) made up of predominantly Hausa men were initially camped on the land where King's College, Lagos is situated."
- Daily Trust: "Formerly, the force was at Kings College near the Tafawa Balewa Square ... members of the force who were largely Hausa ..."

## 3. When the land was needed, the Oba of Lagos pushed for them to be moved
- Wikipedia: "a need arose for the use of the land and the Oba of Lagos pressured the Governor of Lagos to resettle the RWAFF men, and sold virgin land in what is now Obalende to the British Colonial Government."
- Daily Trust: "When development reached that area, Oba Eshugbayi sent somebody to find another place. They found the land now known as Obalende."
- (Script says only "pushed to move them"; no date or governor name claimed.)

## 4. Famous for Suya Junction
- Wikipedia: "Obalende is famous for its night life ... and for its suya with a junction popularly called Suya junction."
- Ofadaa, "Obalende Suya Junction, Ikoyi, Lagos" (listing of the spot) https://ofadaa.com/lagos/restaurants/obalende-suya-junction
- Hotels.ng places listing "Obalende Suya Junction" https://hotels.ng/places/restaurants/3134-obalende-suya-junction
