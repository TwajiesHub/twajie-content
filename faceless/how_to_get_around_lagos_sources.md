## 4. BRT buses
- Claim: Mile 12 to CMS BRT corridor commissioned 17 March 2008 and running on dedicated bus lanes (about 65% physically segregated, 20% marked bus lanes, the rest mixed traffic, so "own lanes" is a simplification).
  - LAMATA, Bus Services: https://www.lamata-ng.com/bus-services/
  - SSATP / World Bank discussion paper, "Lagos Bus Rapid Transit": https://www.ssatp.org/sites/default/files/publication/DP09-Lagos-BRT.pdf
  - World Bank IEG, Lagos Urban Transport Project PPAR: https://ieg.worldbankgroup.org/sites/default/files/Data/reports/ppar_nigeria_0716.pdf
- Map pin: Obalende Bus Terminus 6.4496, 3.4087 (OpenStreetMap Overpass).

## 3. Red Line
- Claim: Agbado to Oyingbo; 27 km; 8 stations; full commercial operations from 15 Oct 2024.
  - Daily Post, 15 Oct 2024: https://dailypost.ng/2024/10/15/lagos-govt-commissions-red-line-rail-commercial-operations/
  - Nairametrics, 11 Oct 2024: https://nairametrics.com/2024/10/11/lagos-red-line-starts-full-passenger-operations-on-tuesday-oct-15/
  - The Guardian Nigeria: https://guardian.ng/news/red-line-begins-operations-in-lagos-on-october-15/
- Map pin: Oyingbo station 6.4834, 3.3815 (OSM).

  - Lagos Television: https://lagostelevision.com/lamata-to-enforce-cashless-bus-fare-payments-from-august-1/
