# Sources: Where is Zuma Rock? (carousel2_zuma, TikTok photo carousel, Sun 22 Nov 2026 1 PM WAT)

## Facts (slide: claim -> sources)
- S1/S2: in Madalla, Suleja LGA, Niger State (not FCT), beside the Abuja–Kaduna road, called "gateway to Abuja" -> Wikipedia 'Zuma Rock' https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zuma_Rock ; "The Security Implication of Neglecting Tourist Attractions in Nigeria: A Case Study of Zuma Rock", Research Square preprint (2022) https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1944156/v1 ; BBC News (in neighbouring Niger State, by the main north-south highway)
- S1: about 40 km from Abuja -> Research Square (2022) ("just about 40 kilometres away from Abuja"); placetogoto.com/places/zuma-rock ("about 40 km from central Abuja", "where the Abuja-Kaduna road leaves the FCT")
- S3: about 300 m (980 ft) above its surroundings -> Atlas Obscura https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/zuma-rock ; Research Square (2022) ; Wikipedia (prominence 300 m). 725 m above sea level -> Wikipedia (cites The Sun, 2008) ; Culture Trip https://theculturetrip.com/africa/nigeria/articles/myths-and-legends-surrounding-nigerias-zuma-rock/
- S4: on the reverse of the N100 note, first issued 1 Dec 1999 -> Wikipedia 'Nigerian naira' (banknote table: reverse = Zuma Rock; issue 1 December 1999) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_naira ; BBC News 'Big banknote too much for Nigeria' (29 Dec 1999) http://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/africa/582502.stm ; Atlas Obscura (on the 100 naira note since 1999)
- S5: caption said FCT Abuja; Niger State threatened to sue the Central Bank; CBN sent a delegation to apologise; later prints removed 'FCT, ABUJA' -> BBC News (29 Dec 1999) ; Wikipedia 'Nigerian naira' ; Research Square (2022) (protest led to erasure of 'FCT Abuja')
- S6: water runoff left marks like a human face (eyes, nose, mouth) -> Atlas Obscura ; Culture Trip
- S7: Zuba people called it 'Zumwa' after the guinea fowls there -> Atlas Obscura ("a place of guinea fowls") ; Culture Trip ("the place of catching guineafowls"). Gbagyi (Gwari) used it as a refuge/hideout in intertribal wars -> Wikipedia 'Zuma Rock' ; Culture Trip ; Atlas Obscura (defensive position)
