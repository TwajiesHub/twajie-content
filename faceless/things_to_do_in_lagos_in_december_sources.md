## 5. Freedom Park, Lagos Island
- Claim: former colonial prison (Her Majesty's Broad Street Prison); Awolowo was held there (on-screen pill); opened Oct 2010 for Nigeria's 50th.
  - Wikipedia, Freedom Park (Lagos): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_Park,_Lagos (formerly Her Majesty's Broad Street Prison; notable prisoners incl. Obafemi Awolowo)
  - Freedom Park Lagos official "About": https://freedomparklagos.com/about.php (born out of the ruins of Her Majesty's Broad Street Prison; opened 1 Oct 2010)
  - The Guardian Nigeria, "The interesting story behind Freedom Park": https://guardian.ng/life/the-interesting-story-behind-freedom-park/ (prison held Herbert Macaulay, Michael Imoudu, Anthony Enahoro, Obafemi Awolowo)
  - P.M. News, 13 Oct 2010: https://pmnewsnigeria.com/2010/10/13/when-ikoyi-prison-became-freedom-park/ (Gov. Fashola named Awolowo among former inmates)
- Map pin: 6.4488, 3.3965 (Wikipedia / OSM Nominatim).

  - IHS Towers press release, 11 Apr 2026: https://www.ihstowers.com/support-and-info/media/press-releases/2026/ihs-nigeria-sponsors-the-renovation-and-upgrade-of-facilities-at
  - National Commission for Museums and Monuments: https://museum.ng/echoes-of-the-past/ (Echoes of the Past opened April 11 at National Museum Lagos)

## 3. Nike Art Gallery, Lekki
- Claim: several floors of art ("floor after floor"); entry is free; thousands of artworks (on-screen pill).
  - Wikipedia, Nike Art Gallery: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nike_Art_Gallery ("Admission is free"; spans four floors)
  - Atlas Obscura: https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/nike-centre-for-art-and-culture (five stories, ~8,000 pieces, free to the public)
  - TravelingNG (2026): https://travelingng.com/nike-art-gallery/ ("Walking in is free"; ~8,000 pieces in the building)
  - The Sun Nigeria: https://thesun.ng/nike-art-gallery-2/ ("Everyone is guaranteed a free entry")
  - Floor count differs between sources (4 vs 5), so the script avoids a number.
- Map pin: 6.4315, 3.4819 (OSM Nominatim).

## 2. Lekki Arts and Crafts Market
- Claim: also called Jakande market (on-screen); prices are bargained ("come ready to haggle").
  - Culture Trip: https://theculturetrip.com/africa/nigeria/lagos/articles/for-the-love-of-nigerian-art-supporting-arts-and-crafts-in-lagos (also known as Oba Elegushi International Market or Jakande Art Market)
  - MarketDays.ng: https://marketdays.ng/lagos-state/eti-osa-lg/jakande-art-market/ (Jakande Art Market = Lekki Arts and Crafts Market)
  - Forbes, 2015, "Lekki Market: Frontier Town of Capitalism": https://www.forbes.com/sites/abrambrown/2015/02/22/lekki-market-frontier-town-of-capitalism/ (also called Jakande Market; business is "bargained, nose to nose")
- Map pin: 6.4343, 3.5054 (OSM "Arts and Craft Market" via Overpass).
