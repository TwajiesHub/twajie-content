# Sources: Where is Obudu Mountain Resort? (carousel1, TikTok photo carousel, Sat 21 Nov 2026 1 PM WAT)

## Facts (slide: claim -> sources)
- S1/S4: about 1,600 m elevation; semi-temperate climate; formerly Obudu Cattle Ranch -> Wikipedia 'Obudu Mountain Resort' https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obudu_Mountain_Resort ; The Guardian Nigeria (1,576 m, temperate climate) https://guardian.ng/saturday-magazine/travel-a-tourism/obudu-mountain-resort-a-destination-for-tourists-lovers/ ; NZZ (top station 1,600 m) https://www.nzz.ch/die_verrueckteste_luftseilbahn_der_welt-ld.577567
- S2: Obudu Plateau, north-east Cross River State, close to Cameroon border -> Wikipedia 'Obudu Mountain Resort'; The Guardian Nigeria (closer to the border of Cameroon)
- S3: about 332 km drive from Calabar -> Wikipedia 'Obudu Mountain Resort'. ~6 hours -> Wikipedia 'Obudu' (6-hour drive from Calabar) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obudu ; NZZ (six hours Calabar to the lower station)
- S4: mist -> viorah.tv guide https://viorah.tv/obudu-mountain-resort/ ; Guardian (reception 'often in the clouds')
- S5: cable car opened 2005, ~4 km (4,030 m), climbs ~870 m, 'one of the longest cable car rides in Africa' -> Obudu Mountain Resort https://www.obuduranchresort.com/article/10/cable-car-ride ; NZZ (4,030 m, 870 m, opened 2005) ; Wikipedia (since 2005, 870 m) ; Guardian ('about the longest in Africa', four km). Outages: Wikipedia Transport section (grounded for a while for lack of maintenance; revival pledged 2020) -> hence 'check it's running'
- S6: race first held 2005, ~11 km up the twisting road to the plateau, US$50,000 to each winner, 'world's richest mountain race' -> World Athletics 'Du Bois captures Mt. Obudu title' (27 Nov 2005) https://worldathletics.org/news/news/du-bois-captures-mt-obudu-title ; World Athletics 'The World's Richest Mountain Race' (2006) https://worldathletics.org/news/news/the-worlds-richest-mountain-race ; Athletics Africa (2009, $50,000 winners) https://www.athletics.africa/www2/news/2009/11/29/ethiopians_sweep_obudu_international_mountain_race_2009/397.html ; Wikipedia 'Obudu Ranch International Mountain Race'. (Worded as 'first held in 2005', not claiming it is held every year now.)
- S7: Scottish rancher McCaughley camped on the Oshie Ridge for a month in 1949; ranch developed 1951 -> Wikipedia 'Obudu Mountain Resort' ; Obudu Mountain Resort About us https://www.obuduranchresort.com/page/48/about-us ; Punch https://punchng.com/uphill-task-cross-rivers-battle-to-restore-obudu-mountains-resort-fading-glory/
