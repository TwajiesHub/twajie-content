# Sources: Best places to propose in Lagos this Christmas (faceless26)
Researched 14 Nov 2026 (WAT). Fees and hours change; on-screen lines only use facts with 2+ sources. Anything else says "check before you go".

## Trigger
Viewer comment on the Lekki canopy walkway video: visiting Lagos at Christmas, wants a public proposal that "a lot of people" see. No username used in the video.

## #5 Tarkwa Bay (Lagos Harbour) - used: "a beach you reach by boat; public boats stop around six"
- Boat-only access: Wikivoyage https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Tarkwa_Bay_(Lagos) ; Nigeria Planet https://nigeria-planet.com/tarkwa-bay-beach-in-lagos ; YonneTours https://yonnetours.com/what-you-need-you-know-about-tarkwa-bay-beach/
- Public boats stop ~6 PM / last boat 6 PM: YonneTours (Apr 2025) "public ferries stop running around 6 PM"; BestLagos https://bestlagos.ng/tarkwa-bay-beach-guide-review/ "The last boat out of the island leaves at 6 pm".
- Jetties: CMS Marina, Mekwe (Bonny Camp), Tarzan (VI), Fiki Marina (several sources above). Fares quoted vary widely (N1,500 to N5,000 public; private far more) -> not used; check before you go.
- Dec sunset in Lagos is ~6:29-6:42 PM (meridian-clock.com/sunrise-sunset/lagos/december), i.e. after the last public boat, so a sunset proposal needs a private boat. Not stated on screen.

## #4 Muri Okunola Park (Victoria Island) - used: "N1,000 entry; book setups via LASPARK"
- LASPARK official park page: lawn without setup N1,000/head; lawn with setup 10-100 people N30,000-300,000; exclusive use N1,600,000. https://lasparkportal.lagosstate.gov.ng/parks/muri-okunola-park/
- LASPARK portal rules: entry N1,000; permits required for events, content creation, professional photography/videography, drones; hours 9 AM-6 PM "unless otherwise authorized". https://lasparkadminportal.lagosstate.gov.ng/
- Second source for N1,000 and booking needed for events/photo sessions: FlightPadi https://flightpadi.com/attraction/muri-okunola-park/ (lists 8 AM-8 PM hours, which conflicts with LASPARK's 9-6; hours not used on screen).

## #3 Lekki Conservation Centre canopy walkway - used: "closes at five; open 8:30 AM to 5 PM; 401 m"
- Hours 8:30-17:00 daily: ucomeafrik 2026 guide https://ucomeafrik.com/blog/lekki-conservation-centre-tour-guide-2026 ; viorah.tv https://viorah.tv/lekki-conservation-centre/ ; Visit Lagos City https://www.visitlagoscity.com/place/lekki-conservation-centre/
- 401 m, billed as Africa's longest canopy walkway: ucomeafrik; viorah.tv ("around 401 metres... billed as the longest of its kind in Africa").
- Fees: adult entry ~N5,000; canopy walk extra, reported N1,500-N5,000 (ucomeafrik fees page says no official fee schedule found). Not used on screen -> check before you go. Tickets at the gate, no online booking (opentravelguide.com).

## #2 Lekki-Ikoyi Link Bridge - used: "at dusk, when the lights come on; stay on the walkway"
- Lit at night / popular for proposals and pre-wedding shoots: Pulse Nigeria https://www.pulse.ng/story/the-lekki-ikoyi-link-bridge-this-is-the-most-photographed-place-in-lagos-2024080307170163094
- Pylon illuminated in colours at night: AS+P (architects) https://www.as-p.com/projects/lekki-ikoyi-link-bridge-291
- Pedestrian sidewalks; stay on the pedestrian path; professional shoots/drones need permission; lighting depends on whether lights are working: TravelingNG (Oct 2026) https://travelingng.com/lekki-ikoyi-link-bridge/ ; sidewalks also in Smart Cities Dive.

## #1 Christmas lights, Ajose Adeogun Street (Victoria Island) - used: "Zenith Bank's yearly light-up; big December crowds"
- Zenith Bank's annual light-up of Ajose Adeogun Street and Roundabout, 18th year in Nov 2024, draws visitors "from far and wide": Punch https://punchng.com/zenith-bank-ushers-in-yuletide-with-lagos-street-light-up/
- 19th year in 2025, ~1 km of lit trees, crowds "too numerous to count", couples visiting: The Nation (31 Dec 2025) https://thenationonlineng.net/light-up-christmas-yuletide-illuminations-transforming-lagos/
- Other Lagos displays in Dec 2025: Falomo under-bridge display, Eko Hotel roundabout trees (The Nation); Maltina light-up on Obafemi Awolowo Way (Vanguard https://www.vanguardngr.com/2025/12/firm-lights-up-lagos-abuja-to-launch-christmas-celebrations/); Africanews https://www.africanews.com/2025/12/19/christmas-lights-attract-lagos-residents-despite-violence-and-rising-costs/
- 2026 switch-on date not announced yet (it was late November in 2024/2025) -> check before you go.

## Excluded
