# Sources: Who is Carter Bridge named after? (faceless25)
- Wikipedia "Carter Bridge": built 1901; named after Sir Gilbert Thomas Carter, former Governor of the Colony of Lagos; Iddo to Idumota; one of three bridges linking Lagos Island and mainland. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carter_Bridge
- Pulse Nigeria, "Inside the History of Carter Bridge": named after Carter, Governor of Lagos Colony; completed 1901; Iddo to Idumota. https://www.pulse.ng/story/carter-bridge-history-lagos-125-year-old-bridge-2026030712413438160
- The Engineer-era Lagos Railway article (wasc.org.uk PDF): Carter-road bridge "for light carts and foot passengers only", named after the late governor Sir Gilbert Carter, connecting Lagos with Iddo; steam tramway planned over it. https://www.wasc.org.uk/Nigeria%20history%20docs/Railway/Lagos%20Railway%2019010628.pdf
- Cambridge Univ. Press "Troubled waters" (Urban History): Carter Bridge completed 1901 and the steam tram extended across it. https://doi.org/10.1017/s0963926825100321
- IFRA "Milestones in urban infrastructure, Lagos": 1901 opening of Carter Bridge. https://doi.org/10.4000/books.ifra.833
